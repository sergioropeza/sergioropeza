**Backend developer · Odoo & iDempiere ERP**

🇬🇧 I build and customize ERP systems for companies in Latin America: Odoo modules, iDempiere plugins, reports, integrations and the PostgreSQL logic underneath.

🇪🇸 Desarrollo y personalizo ERPs para empresas de Latinoamérica: módulos de Odoo, plugins de iDempiere, reportes, integraciones y la lógica en PostgreSQL que los sostiene.

## What I work on · En qué trabajo

**Odoo** (17 / 19, Community & Enterprise, Odoo.sh)
- Custom modules: sales, purchase, stock and accounting
- Latin American fiscal localizations and electronic invoicing
- QWeb reports, including overlays for pre-printed forms
- Integrations with external systems

**iDempiere** (10 / 12 / 13)
- OSGi plugins: processes, model validators, ZK forms with custom JavaScript
- Financial and tax reporting with PL/pgSQL engines and JasperReports
- ZK themes and UI improvements
